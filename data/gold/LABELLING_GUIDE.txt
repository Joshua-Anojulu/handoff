CANONICAL VALUE SPACE, revision 2 (use exactly these strings)

Revision 2 responds to the round-1 annotator's findings. Changes:
  * medication gains four second-phase fields, so a phased regimen
    ('10 mg BID for 7 days, then 5 mg BID for 6 months') no longer
    loses its second half:
      then_dose, then_frequency, then_duration_days, then_duration_note
    then_duration_note is "stop" for '... then stop', else as before.
  * red_flag.threshold now carries direction and unit, not a bare
    number: "over 101 F", "below 70 mg/dL", "over 3 pounds",
    "over 10 minutes", "over 1 pads". Direction is "over" or "below".
  * restriction gains `condition` for the clause that outlives the
    number: "while taking oxycodone", "unassisted", "unsupervised",
    "until symptom free". Empty string when there is none.
  * new follow-up roles, new restriction activities, new symptoms
    (listed below). 'one-sided weakness' is renamed to
    'weakness or numbness'.
  * 'seek immediate medical attention' is now action="emergency",
    not "call urgently". It means present for care, not telephone.
