# note: cabg
# synthetic. coronary artery bypass grafting, postoperative day 5.
CARDIAC SURGERY DISCHARGE INSTRUCTIONS

Procedure: coronary artery bypass grafting x 3, with saphenous vein harvest from the left leg.

MEDICATIONS
- Aspirin 81 mg PO QD indefinitely. Do not stop this without speaking to your surgeon.
- Metoprolol tartrate 25 mg PO BID.
- Atorvastatin 80 mg PO QHS.
- Lisinopril 5 mg PO QD.
- Furosemide 20 mg PO QD for 7 days, then stop.
- Potassium chloride 20 mEq PO QD for 7 days.
- Acetaminophen 1000 mg PO q8h scheduled.
- Oxycodone 5 mg PO q6h PRN severe pain. Not more than 4 tablets in 24 hours.
- Docusate 100 mg PO BID.

STERNAL PRECAUTIONS - OBSERVE FOR 8 WEEKS
- No lifting, pushing, or pulling anything over 10 pounds.
- Do not push up from a chair with your arms. Use your legs.
- Do not reach behind your back or above your head.
- No driving for 6 weeks.

INCISION CARE
- Shower daily. Do not soak in a bath until cleared.
- Do not apply lotion or powder to the sternal incision or the leg incision.
- Wear the compression stocking on the harvest leg during the day. Remove at night.

ACTIVITY
- Walk daily, increasing by 5 minutes each day as tolerated.
- Elevate the harvest leg when seated.
- Cardiac rehabilitation begins in 4 weeks.

FOLLOW-UP
- Follow up with cardiac surgery in 2 weeks.
- Follow up with cardiology in 4 weeks.
- Follow up with your primary care physician in 3 weeks.

CALL THE CARDIAC SURGERY OFFICE IMMEDIATELY FOR:
- Temperature above 101 F
- Sternal instability, a clicking or shifting sensation in the breastbone
- Erythema, induration, or purulent drainage from either incision
- Weight gain over 3 pounds in one day
- Palpitations or an irregular heartbeat
- Increasing dyspnea or peripheral edema

CALL 911 FOR CHEST PAIN, SEVERE DYSPNEA, OR SYNCOPE.
