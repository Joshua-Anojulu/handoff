# note: copd-exac
# synthetic. COPD exacerbation, steroid taper plus antibiotic course.
CHRONIC OBSTRUCTIVE PULMONARY DISEASE: DISCHARGE PLAN

Admitted for an acute exacerbation. Treated with systemic corticosteroid, nebulised bronchodilator, and antibiotic.

MEDICATIONS
- Prednisone 40 mg PO QD for 5 days, then stop. Do not taper.
- Doxycycline 100 mg PO BID for 5 days.
- Albuterol inhaler 2 puffs q4h PRN dyspnea.
- Fluticasone-salmeterol inhaler 1 puff BID. Rinse your mouth after each use.
- Pantoprazole 40 mg PO QD while on prednisone.

OXYGEN
- Continuous home oxygen at 2 litres per minute by nasal cannula.
- Do not smoke, and do not permit smoking near the oxygen concentrator. This is a fire hazard.

PULMONARY REHABILITATION
- Pulmonary rehabilitation referral has been placed. The programme will contact you within 2 weeks.
- Perform deep breathing exercises every hour while awake.

FOLLOW-UP
- Follow up with pulmonology in 10 days.
- Follow up with your primary care physician in 5 days.

WARNING SIGNS - call the pulmonary service right away:
- Increasing dyspnea despite rescue inhaler use
- Change in sputum colour or volume
- Temperature above 100.8 F
- Peripheral edema
- Somnolence or confusion, which may indicate hypercapnia

Call 911 for dyspnea at rest that does not improve with the rescue inhaler.
