# note: post-op-chole
# synthetic. laparoscopic cholecystectomy, uncomplicated.
DISCHARGE INSTRUCTIONS

MEDICATIONS
- Cephalexin 500 mg PO QID x 7 days. Complete the full course.
- Oxycodone 5 mg PO q4-6h PRN severe pain. Do not exceed 6 tablets in 24 hours.
- Acetaminophen 650 mg PO q6h PRN mild pain. Maximum 3000 mg in 24 hours.
- Docusate 100 mg PO BID while taking opioids.

ACTIVITY
- No lifting greater than 10 pounds for 4 weeks.
- Do not drive while taking oxycodone.
- No submersion bathing until the staples are out. Showers are permitted after 48 hours.
- Resume work as tolerated after 2 weeks.

FOLLOW-UP
- Follow up with your surgeon in 14 days for staple removal.
- Contact your primary care physician within 1 week for medication review.

Call the clinic immediately for any of the following:
- Fever over 101 F
- Erythema spreading from the incision
- Purulent drainage
- Dyspnea or chest pain
- Emesis that prevents oral intake
