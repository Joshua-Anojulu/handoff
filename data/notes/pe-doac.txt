# synthetic. submassive pulmonary embolism, discharged on apixaban.
DISCHARGE INSTRUCTIONS: PULMONARY EMBOLISM

You were admitted with a pulmonary embolism. You are being discharged on an oral anticoagulant.

ANTICOAGULATION - THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT PART OF THESE INSTRUCTIONS
- Apixaban 10 mg PO BID for 7 days, then 5 mg PO BID for at least 6 months.
- Do not miss a dose. If you miss one, take it as soon as you remember on the same day, then resume the normal schedule. Do not double a dose.
- Do not stop this medication for any reason without speaking to the prescriber, including before dental work.

OTHER MEDICATIONS
- Acetaminophen 650 mg PO q6h PRN pleuritic pain.
- Do not take ibuprofen, naproxen, or aspirin while on apixaban.

ACTIVITY
- Ambulate as tolerated. Mobility reduces the risk of further clot.
- No strenuous exercise for 2 weeks.
- Avoid prolonged immobility. On any journey over 2 hours, stand and walk every hour.
- No air travel for 2 weeks.

FOLLOW-UP
- Follow up with the anticoagulation clinic in 7 days.
- Follow up with pulmonology in 4 weeks.
- Follow up with haematology in 6 weeks for thrombophilia evaluation.

CALL 911 OR GO TO THE NEAREST EMERGENCY DEPARTMENT FOR:
- Sudden worsening dyspnea
- Chest pain
- Syncope
- Haemoptysis
- Bleeding that will not stop after 15 minutes of direct pressure
- Any head injury, however minor

Call the anticoagulation clinic for unexplained bruising, bleeding gums, blood in the urine, or black stool.
