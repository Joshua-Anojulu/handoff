# note: appendectomy
# synthetic. laparoscopic appendectomy for uncomplicated appendicitis.
POST-OPERATIVE INSTRUCTIONS: LAPAROSCOPIC APPENDECTOMY

Pain control
- Ibuprofen 400 mg PO q6h PRN. Take with food.
- Acetaminophen 1000 mg PO q8h PRN. Do not exceed 3000 mg per day.
- Oxycodone 5 mg PO q6h PRN breakthrough pain only. Limit to 4 tablets in 24 hours.

Bowel regimen
- Docusate 100 mg PO BID.
- Polyethylene glycol 17 g PO QD PRN constipation.

Wound care
- Keep the dressing dry for 48 hours, then remove it.
- Do not get the incision wet in a bath or pool until the wound is fully healed.
- The adhesive strips will separate on their own. Do not peel them off.

Activity restrictions
- No lifting over 15 pounds for 3 weeks.
- No strenuous exercise or sports for 4 weeks.
- Do not drive for 5 days, and not at all while taking oxycodone.
- Ambulate several times daily beginning today.

Follow-up
- Follow up with your surgeon in 2 weeks.

Call the surgical office right away for fever over 100.4 F, erythema or induration at a port site, purulent drainage, abdominal pain that is worsening rather than improving, or emesis that prevents oral intake. Present to the emergency department for severe abdominal pain with rigidity.
