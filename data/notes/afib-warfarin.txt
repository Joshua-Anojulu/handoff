# note: afib-warfarin
# synthetic. new-onset atrial fibrillation, anticoagulation initiated.
DISCHARGE INSTRUCTIONS

You were admitted with new-onset atrial fibrillation. Rate control was achieved and anticoagulation has been initiated for stroke prophylaxis.

MEDICATIONS
- Warfarin 5 mg PO QHS. Your dose will be titrated according to INR.
- Metoprolol tartrate 25 mg PO BID.
- Pantoprazole 40 mg PO QD for gastric prophylaxis.

ANTICOAGULATION PRECAUTIONS
- Do not take aspirin, ibuprofen, or naproxen. These potentiate bleeding.
- Maintain a consistent intake of vitamin K containing foods. Do not abruptly increase or decrease leafy green vegetables.
- Abstain from alcohol.
- Use an electric razor and a soft toothbrush.

FOLLOW-UP
- The anticoagulation clinic will check your INR in 3 days.
- Follow up with cardiology in 2 weeks.
- Follow up with your primary care physician in 4 weeks.

SEEK IMMEDIATE MEDICAL ATTENTION FOR:
- Bleeding that will not stop after 10 minutes of pressure
- Blood in the urine or stool, or black tarry stool
- A severe headache, or any head injury however minor
- New weakness or numbness on one side
- Syncope

Call the anticoagulation clinic for any missed warfarin dose. Do not double a dose to compensate.
