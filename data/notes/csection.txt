# note: csection
# synthetic. cesarean delivery, uncomplicated, postpartum day 3.
POSTPARTUM DISCHARGE INSTRUCTIONS FOLLOWING CESAREAN DELIVERY

MEDICATIONS
- Ibuprofen 600 mg PO q6h scheduled for 72 hours, then PRN. Take with food.
- Acetaminophen 1000 mg PO q8h scheduled. Do not exceed 3000 mg in 24 hours.
- Oxycodone 5 mg PO q6h PRN severe pain. Limit to 3 tablets per day. Both oxycodone and ibuprofen are compatible with breastfeeding.
- Docusate 100 mg PO BID.
- Ferrous sulfate 325 mg PO QD with vitamin C for postpartum anaemia.
- Prenatal vitamin 1 tablet PO QD. Continue while breastfeeding.

INCISION CARE
- Keep the incision clean and dry. Pat dry after showering.
- Do not submerge in a bath or pool until your postpartum visit.
- Support the incision with a pillow when coughing.

ACTIVITY
- No lifting anything heavier than your baby for 6 weeks.
- No driving for 2 weeks, and not while taking oxycodone.
- No sexual activity until cleared at your postpartum visit.
- No strenuous exercise for 6 weeks.
- Rest when the baby rests.

FOLLOW-UP
- Follow up with obstetrics in 2 weeks for an incision check.
- Postpartum visit with obstetrics in 6 weeks.
- The lactation consultant will call you in 3 days.

CALL THE OBSTETRIC SERVICE IMMEDIATELY FOR:
- Temperature above 100.4 F
- Soaking more than one pad an hour, or passing clots larger than a golf ball
- Erythema, induration, or purulent drainage at the incision
- Unilateral calf pain or edema
- Severe headache with visual change, or epigastric pain, which may indicate postpartum preeclampsia
- Feelings of hopelessness or thoughts of harming yourself or the baby. Call at any hour.
