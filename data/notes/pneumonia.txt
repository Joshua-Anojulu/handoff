# synthetic. community-acquired pneumonia, discharged on oral antibiotics.
PATIENT DISCHARGE INSTRUCTIONS - RESPIRATORY

Diagnosis: community-acquired pneumonia, right lower lobe.

MEDICATIONS
1. Amoxicillin-clavulanate 875 mg PO BID for 7 days. Do not discontinue early even if symptoms resolve.
2. Azithromycin 250 mg PO QD for 4 more days.
3. Acetaminophen 650 mg PO q6h PRN fever or pleuritic pain.
4. Albuterol inhaler, 2 puffs q4h PRN wheezing or dyspnea.

SUPPORTIVE CARE
- Use the incentive spirometer 10 times every hour while awake.
- Maintain hydration.
- Avoid smoking. Nicotine cessation resources are attached.

ACTIVITY
- No strenuous exertion for 2 weeks.
- Return to work when you have been afebrile for 48 hours.

FOLLOW-UP
- Follow up with your primary care physician in 5 days.
- A repeat chest radiograph is indicated in 6 weeks.

RETURN TO THE EMERGENCY DEPARTMENT IMMEDIATELY FOR:
- Dyspnea at rest
- Temperature above 102 F
- Confusion or lethargy
- Hemoptysis
- Chest pain
