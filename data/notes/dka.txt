# note: dka
# synthetic. diabetic ketoacidosis resolved, new basal-bolus insulin regimen.
DIABETES DISCHARGE INSTRUCTIONS

You were admitted in diabetic ketoacidosis, which has resolved. You are being discharged on a subcutaneous insulin regimen.

INSULIN
- Insulin glargine 18 units subcutaneous QHS. This is your long-acting insulin and must be taken every night even if you are not eating.
- Insulin lispro 6 units subcutaneous TID with meals. Omit the dose if you skip the meal.

OTHER MEDICATIONS
- Metformin 500 mg PO BID with food.
- Lisinopril 5 mg PO QD.

MONITORING
- Check capillary blood glucose four times a day: before each meal and at bedtime.
- Check urine ketones if blood glucose exceeds 250 mg/dL.
- Record all values and bring the log to every appointment.

SICK DAY RULES
- Do not omit basal insulin when you are unwell, even if you are not eating.
- Maintain hydration with sugar-free fluids.
- Check glucose every 4 hours while unwell.

FOLLOW-UP
- Follow up with endocrinology in 7 days.
- Diabetes education appointment in 3 days.
- Follow up with your primary care physician in 2 weeks.
- Laboratory visit for HbA1c in 3 months.

Call the diabetes service immediately for persistent vomiting, blood glucose above 300 mg/dL on two consecutive checks, positive urine ketones, or blood glucose below 70 mg/dL that does not correct with oral carbohydrate. Call 911 for confusion or lethargy.
