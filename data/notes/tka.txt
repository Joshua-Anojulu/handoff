# synthetic. total knee arthroplasty, discharged to home with PT.
TOTAL KNEE ARTHROPLASTY: DISCHARGE INSTRUCTIONS

MEDICATIONS
- Enoxaparin 40 mg subcutaneous QD for 14 days for thromboprophylaxis.
- Acetaminophen 1000 mg PO q8h scheduled, not PRN.
- Oxycodone 5 mg PO q4h PRN pain. Maximum 8 tablets in 24 hours.
- Ondansetron 4 mg sublingual q8h PRN nausea.
- Senna 8.6 mg PO BID.

WOUND CARE
- The dressing is waterproof. Showers are permitted. Do not submerge the knee.
- Do not apply any ointment or cream to the incision.

ACTIVITY AND WEIGHT-BEARING
- Weight-bearing as tolerated with the walker.
- Perform the prescribed range of motion exercises 3 times a day.
- Elevate the extremity when seated.
- Apply ice for 20 minutes every 2 hours while awake.
- Do not drive for 6 weeks or while taking oxycodone.
- No stairs unassisted for 2 weeks.

FOLLOW-UP
- Physical therapy begins in 2 days and continues 3 times weekly.
- Follow up with orthopedics in 3 weeks.

Contact the orthopedic service immediately if you develop calf pain, unilateral lower extremity edema, fever above 101 F, wound dehiscence, purulent drainage, or erythema spreading from the incision. Call 911 for acute dyspnea or chest pain, which may indicate pulmonary embolism.
