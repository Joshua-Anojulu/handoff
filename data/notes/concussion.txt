# note: concussion
# synthetic. mild traumatic brain injury, negative CT, discharged from ED.
EMERGENCY DEPARTMENT DISCHARGE: MILD TRAUMATIC BRAIN INJURY

Your CT of the head showed no acute intracranial abnormality. You have sustained a concussion.

MEDICATIONS
- Acetaminophen 650 mg PO q6h PRN headache. Do not exceed 3000 mg in 24 hours.
- Ondansetron 4 mg PO q8h PRN nausea.
- Do not take ibuprofen, naproxen, or aspirin for 72 hours. These increase the risk of intracranial bleeding.

COGNITIVE AND PHYSICAL REST
- Relative rest for 48 hours, then graded return to activity as symptoms permit.
- No screens for 24 hours, then limit screen exposure as tolerated.
- No return to sport, contact activity, or any activity with a fall risk until cleared by a physician. A second impact before recovery can be catastrophic.
- No driving until you are symptom free and off all sedating medication.
- Abstain from alcohol for 2 weeks.
- No strenuous exercise until cleared.

SLEEP
- You may sleep normally. You do not need to be woken hourly.
- A responsible adult should remain with you for the first 24 hours.

FOLLOW-UP
- Follow up with your primary care physician in 3 days.
- Follow up with neurology in 2 weeks if symptoms persist.

RETURN TO THE EMERGENCY DEPARTMENT IMMEDIATELY FOR:
- A headache that is severe or progressively worsening
- Repeated emesis
- Seizure activity
- Confusion, disorientation, or difficulty rousing
- Unequal pupils, diplopia, or slurred speech
- New weakness or numbness
- Clear fluid or blood from the nose or ears
