# note: pyelonephritis
# synthetic. acute pyelonephritis treated with IV then oral antibiotics.
DISCHARGE INSTRUCTIONS - ACUTE PYELONEPHRITIS

Diagnosis: acute pyelonephritis, left kidney. Urine culture grew E. coli sensitive to ciprofloxacin.

Medications
- Ciprofloxacin 500 mg PO BID for 10 days. Complete the full course even once you feel well.
- Acetaminophen 650 mg PO q6h PRN fever or flank pain.
- Ondansetron 4 mg PO q8h PRN nausea.

Precautions while taking ciprofloxacin
- Do not take antacids, calcium, or iron within 2 hours of a dose. They impair absorption.
- Avoid prolonged sun exposure. This antibiotic causes photosensitivity.
- Report any tendon pain immediately and discontinue the drug.

Supportive care
- Increase oral fluid intake to at least 2 litres daily.
- Void frequently and completely.

Activity
- No strenuous exercise for 1 week.
- You may return to work in 3 days if afebrile.

Follow-up
- Follow up with your primary care physician in 4 days.
- Repeat urine culture in 2 weeks.
- A renal ultrasound has been ordered; the imaging department will call you.

Return to the emergency department for temperature above 101 F after 48 hours of antibiotics, intractable vomiting preventing oral intake, worsening flank pain, hypotension or near-syncope, or decreased urine output.
